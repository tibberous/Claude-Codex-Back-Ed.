Codex Black Ed.

Gida tun farko multi-provider AI chat panel don VSCode. Danna Ctrl+Alt+B don tunkarar.

1. Maraba

Codex Black wata VSCode webview ce wanda ke ba ka damar yin magana da masu samarwa AI da dama (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, Azure, gida Ollama) daga panel guda, mai kamannewa. Kome da ke buƙatar maɓalɓali yana karanta daga config.ini a asusun jaji — babu buƙatar extension settings UI, amma Settings modal yana nuna abubuwa mafi ƙamari masu yoron baje dole.

Saba ne an zo? Fara ta bubuɗe Tsaita (rago), zaɓi masu samarwa, liƙa maɓallacin API, sai ku rubuta abken kuma bugi Enter. Kome sauran aikin da ke nan gida wannan jagora, fasalin ne.

3. Akwatin abken

Babbar editable textarea a gida. Rubuta, liƙa, ja fayil, haɗi ta ƙugiya — kome da kuke saka a nan shine ba da fulanin gida na gaggawa API na gida.

Haɗi an ƙunshi ga VSCode editor's monospace haɗi (ta yadda --vscode-editor-font-family CSS variable) haka panel yana duba ko kanta a ciki editor.

5. Models & masu samarwa

CBE yana aiki da 11 karuwa azaman drop-in .bridge plugins karkashin bridges/. Kowane daya wata zip ce ta kai ɗaya tare da manifest.xml + bridge.py + abubuwan kalangu. Saɓa saba .bridge fayil kuma yana bayyana a cartridge zaɓon — babu buƙatar jaji code canji.

Mauna-chat karuwa (7)

  • ChatGPT — chatgpt.com, Plus/Pro subscription
  • Grok — grok.com, xAI shiga (anonymous tier yana rashin gida; shiga kaɗai)
  • Gemini — gemini.google.com, Google akaunt
  • Claude — claude.ai, magic-link shiga (gudna tools/sign_in_helper.py claude kaɗai)
  • Copilot — copilot.microsoft.com, free Microsoft akaunt
  • DeepSeek — chat.deepseek.com, gida email/password (kama ba yi amfani Google SSO ba)
  • Ollama — gida 127.0.0.1:11434, babu shiga buƙatu

Bideo-samarwa karuwa (4)

  • Sora 2 — sora.com, yadda saidawa ChatGPT Plus/Pro shiga
  • Google Veo — gemini.google.com (Veo yanayi), Gemini Advanced subscription
  • Runway Gen-3/4 — app.runwayml.com, daban Runway subscription
  • Bing Video Creator — bing.com/videos/create, free Sora-2 tare da Microsoft akaunt

Abubuwan bideo da aka samarwa suna adana zuwa videos/<bridge>/<timestamp>_<prompt-slug>.mp4 kuma auto-play a Video Library panel (duba §Video Library a gida).

Zaɓi karuwa + model daga cartridge zaɓon kusa da akwatin abken, ko ta Tsaita → General. Zaɓon yana gida per-workspace kuma ta adana a workspaceState. Don ƙari karuwa saba, duba docs/WRITING_BRIDGES.md.

5a. Video Library

Ka tawaɗe panel don kirga + duba bideona da aka samarwa ta karuwa bideo (Sora, Veo, Runway, Bing Video Creator). Bubuɗe ta yadda Command Palette: Codex Black: Open Video Library ko codexBlackEd.openVideoPlayer aiki.

  • Sidebar jera kome .mp4 / .webm / .mov karkashin videos/<bridge>/, karuwa da tsarai kaɗai-gida.
  • Sabbin bideo auto-play a bubuɗen farko.
  • Panel yana kula da videos/ dir a duba 2-makyari — kome samarwa bideo saba yana bayyana tare da ✨ "Jiya samarwa" badge kuma auto-plays (toggle off ta Auto ▶ new).
  • Danna 📂 Bubuɗe don nuna videos/ folda a OS fayil manager.

6. Iska zuwa mauna

Danna mic, yi magana, danna gida don tsayawa (ko jira da sura-timeout). Injina biyu ana ƙoƙarin a jeri:

  1. Web Speech API — Google's gida recognizer da ke aiki a webview. Yana amfani da Gmail/Google saɓon; babu maɓallaci saba da aka buƙata.
  2. Windows SAPI (karamshe) — pre-warmed PowerShell aiki wanda aka kira lokacin webview blocks mic amfani. Configurable sura window ta [voice] listen_seconds a config.ini.
SAPI ne pre-warmed a extension activate haka farko mic danna yana gida 2-4 makyari sannu da aka ƙare wacce ta kumce farko tsayawa-danna.

7. Auto Prompt

Badge-tare-ta-spinner batatu. Lokacin ON, bayan N makyari na rashin aiki panel auto-send mauna ta auto-wake abken. Countdown digit ana zana a --cbe-highlight-color, wanda kome skin yana bayyana.

Baje mauna ta 🔁 Wake batatu — yana zagaye wake.txt.

8. Auto karanta (TTS)

Bakaki aiki. Lokacin ON, kome mai karatu amsa ana magana a ta Web Speech synthesizer (Google) ko SAPI karamshe. Baba shuɗi halo yana koma circle aiki yayin sauti yana aiki.

9. Karama & amfani kontekst

Jiga array aiki zuwa model yana girma tare da kome sauni. Ring kewaye Karama icon nuna amfani kontekst-gida na yanzu azaman conic gradient — kunya lokacin saba, cikakke lokacin kusa da model's kontekst iyaka.

Danna Karama don raɓa tarihin zuwa daga sauni hudu. Gaggawa API na gida yana amfani ƙaramin array.

10. Amsa tsarewa

Adana a wake.txt a asusun jaji. An amfani ta ta Auto Prompt azaman sauni don aiki lokacin rashin aiki timer yana fito. Baje ta 🔁 toolbar batatu.

11. Haɗin fayiloli

Danna 📎 don zaɓi faili ɗaya ko da yawa. Rike Ctrl ko Shift a zaɓo don multi-zaɓo. Kome fayil ana tsara cikin abken azaman fenced code block tare da sunan fayil da harshe. Fayiloli daban aboƙin mahaidorin 1 MB ana tsiyaye tare da sanarwa.

12. Git wuri

Danna 📁 Git. Idan babu aikace folda da aka tsara, kuna samun folda zaɓo kaɗai — zaɓon hanya ana rubuta zuwa [project] path a config.ini. Sauni git na gida yana aiki tare da cwd sa zuwa hanya.

13. GitHub gida

Jera abubuwan da kamar yadda [github] pat. Danna layi don tsara git URL zuwa abken. Jera yana paginate kuma cached.

14. NameSilo Yankunan

Ja kome yankun a NameSilo akaunt ta amfani [namesilo] api_key (ko [api_keys] namesilo_api_key azaman karamshe). A farko bubuɗen, sakamakon cached zuwa domains.txt; sauni panel boots serve daga cache. Danna Reload a wuri don abin saba ja.

Idan kun taɓa ga "babu yankunan" tare da 300 amsa code, duba trace tashar — kunya caches ba ta gida ba poisoning gida boots ba, amma saba da domains.txt a sauran nuni hanya kula.

15. Jiki & jiya

Batatu 🎨 pop gano zaɓo wanda yana kula skins/ jiya (ba a startup), haka saɓa saba folda a aiki ya isa. Kome jiki yana da manifest.xml wanda yana bayyana palette — har da highlight kala, wanda Auto Prompt countdown badge, focus rings, kuma supervisor pulse duka amfani.

Jiki da suka kai da sun hada: gnome (Adwaita gray), terminal (black/green tare da hot-pink accent), tamagotchi (egg-shell pastels tare da sprite kalangu panel), blocky, da sauran.

16. Tsaita

Bubuɗe daga ⚙ rago. Tabs:

  • General — nuna suna, default model, send-on-enter
  • Voice — mic device, listen_seconds, STT injina zaɓo, TTS auto-karanta
  • Updates — is_admin flag, WinSCP saɓon, remote hanya
  • Maɓallaci — kome samarwa maɓallaci API
  • Monitor — VSCode mai kula tunkarar, port, poll interval
  • Harshe — zaɓi daga 39 locales; duba § 17

Wuri yana tuna girman sa kuma sassa tsakanin saɓo.

17. Harshe

Panel yana aiki da fassarori don 39 mafi-yi-magana harsunan. Locale fayiloli zaɓo a languages/<code>.xml kuma loading kaɗai a extension activate. Bambanta maɓuɓɓug ta karamshe zuwa English.

Zaɓi harshe a Tsaita → Harshe. Rago aiki ana nuna kusa da kome zaɓo (loading daga assets/flags/<code>.svg). Zaɓon ana adana zuwa [settings] language a config.ini.

18. VSCode mai kula

A Windows wasaici (CBEVSCodeSupervisor) yana kebe tools/vscode_supervisor.ps1 script ta NSSM kuma yana ɗaura HttpListener a http://127.0.0.1:3434/. Panel's monitor batatu yana hasken baba lokacin mai kula /status endpoint yana amsa 200 OK.

Idan HTTP probe yana gazawa amma wasaici ya nuna shidar, panel's watchdog auto-restart wasaici (30-makyari cool-down haka ba restart-storm ba).

Haragu-danna monitor batatu don nuni / farko / tsayawa. Kyauta-danna don bubuɗe trace tashar.

19. Auto-update (ja)

4 makyari bayan jaji activate, panel yana fetch https://trentontompkins.com/cbe/manifest.xml.php — MD5+girma manifest na sabbin jajiya fayiloli. Kome remote fayil, gida copy's MD5 ana kwatanta; kawai yadda/bambanta fayiloli ana down a HTTPS.

User-gida ƙasa (config.ini, debug.log, domains.txt, wake.txt, prompt_history.txt) na ja exclusion jeri kuma ba ta share kuma ba.

20. Admin push

Kawai aboƙai tare da [updates] is_admin = true sai su push zuwa wasaici. Wuce ta:

  • Command Palette → Codex Black: Push update
  • Slash aiki /push

Yana amfani WinSCP adana saɓon da suka rubuta a [updates] winscp_session (default vps). Pushes ta /script=… tare da synchronize aiki kuma filemask wanda yana toshe siri & per-machine ƙasa.

21. Extensions kasuwa

Danna 🧩 Extensions batatu. Iframe wuri yana bubuɗe zuwa https://trentontompkins.com/cbe/extension/extension_marketplace.php, wanda yana fetch catalog a extensions.xml.php kuma zana kome extension yadda kadi tare da bayanai, tags, version, fayil girma, MD5, kuma Install batatu.

Yadda ya zanga azaman <id>.ext — zip archive wanda ya dauke manifest.xml kuma yadda code (talakakkeextension.html). Lokacin ku danna Install, kasuwa iframe yana aiki postMessage zuwa panel; panel yana download .ext fayil kai tsaye daga wasaici kuma tsara shi karkashin extensions/ a jaji folda.

Demo yadda ukku ya aiki tare da launch catalog: Calculator, Minesweeper, kuma Emoji Picker.

22. Slash aiki

Rubuta / a akwatin abken don bubuɗe slash-aiki paleta. Danna layi don tsara aiki mauna cikin abken (editable), ko bugi Enter a aladun layi don fito shi kai.

23. Kyauta dama menu

Kyauta-danna kuma a kume a panel don wuri menu (bayyana azaman gida XML string a panel.js). Abubuwan da suka kai Refresh (reload panel ba taɓuɗe jaji host — kada ka yi amfani reloadWindow), Gadi jiga, Bubuɗe tsaita, kuma Inspect (DevTools, debug builds kawai).

24. Trace tashar

Jaji yana rubuta kaifi timeline zuwa debug.log kusa da jaji. Kome abubuwan da suka saba ya zo ciki: samarwa switches, STT lifecycle, mai kula probes, NameSilo amsa codes, panel↔host bridge sauti, push/pull manifest jera.

Kyauta-danna monitor batatu don kaɗai-danna bubuɗen trace tashar.
